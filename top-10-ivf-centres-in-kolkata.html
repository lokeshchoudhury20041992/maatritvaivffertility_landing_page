📌 Quick Summary

When comparing IVF centres in Kolkata, check the doctors' recognised qualifications and medical registration, the clinic's registration under the ART (Regulation) Act, 2021, embryology lab standards, a written, itemised cost estimate, and outcome data broken down by age. IVF treatment cost in Kolkata is indicatively ₹90,000 – ₹2,50,000 per cycle depending on the procedures needed. Outcomes vary widely between individuals, and no clinic can guarantee a pregnancy.

Why Choosing the Right IVF Centre in Kolkata Matters

Infertility affects around 1 in 6 people worldwide (WHO, 2023). IVF involves several steps, and a number of clinic-level factors can affect your experience and care. Points worth looking at include:

  • Embryology lab standards (air quality, culture media, incubator technology)
  • Doctor experience with cases similar to yours (low AMH, previous unsuccessful cycles, age)
  • Individualised treatment protocols based on your assessment
  • Available procedures (ICSI, blastocyst culture, PGT-A, ERA) and when they are actually indicated
  • Clear, written pricing that states what is and is not included

This guide does not rank clinics. It sets out what to ask so that you can compare centres yourself.

✅ What to Compare When Choosing an IVF Centre

1

Doctor qualifications and registration

Check each doctor's recognised qualifications and their State Medical Council or National Medical Register registration. Ask who will personally manage your stimulation, egg retrieval and embryo transfer, and how much experience they have with cases like yours.

2

Clinic registration and lab standards

ART clinics and banks in India must be registered under the Assisted Reproductive Technology (Regulation) Act, 2021. Ask to see the registration, and ask about the embryology lab's set-up, quality-control processes and the qualifications of the embryologists.

3

Outcome data you can actually compare

Ask for the clinic's own results for your age group, and check whether figures are per embryo transfer, per cycle started or cumulative, and whether they refer to pregnancy or live birth. Be cautious of headline figures given without this context, or of any promise of success.

4

Written, itemised cost estimate

Ask for a written estimate that lists consultations, investigations, medications, procedures, embryo freezing and storage, and what happens to costs if a cycle is cancelled.

5

Counselling, consent and embryo-transfer policy

Ask what counselling and support is offered, how informed consent is taken, and how many embryos the clinic usually transfers. Single embryo transfer reduces the risk of multiple pregnancy.

📊 How Age Affects IVF Success

Female age is the strongest single factor in IVF outcomes. The figures below are approximate general ranges for illustration only. They are not this clinic's results and are not a prediction for any individual:

Under 35 Years

55% – 70%

Outcomes are generally most favourable in this age group.

Age 35 – 40

40% – 50%

Chances decline progressively; PGT may be discussed in some cases.

Above 40

20% – 35%

Chances with own eggs are lower; donor eggs may be discussed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How should I compare IVF success rates between clinics?

Ask each clinic for its own outcome data broken down by age group, stating whether figures are per embryo transfer, per cycle started or cumulative, and whether they refer to pregnancy or live birth. Headline figures without this context are not comparable. Your own chances depend mainly on age, ovarian reserve, embryo quality, uterine factors, sperm factors and treatment history.

What is the cost of IVF in Kolkata?

IVF treatment in Kolkata typically costs ₹90,000 to ₹2,50,000 per cycle. The exact cost depends on patient age, procedures needed (ICSI, PGT, ERA), and the clinic chosen. Ask for a written, itemised estimate before starting.

How many IVF cycles are usually required?

Many couples need more than one IVF cycle or embryo transfer. The number needed varies with age, ovarian reserve, embryo quality and the underlying cause of infertility, and some couples do not conceive despite treatment. Your doctor can discuss what is realistic in your situation.

Is IVF painful?

Most steps of IVF are minimally invasive. Egg retrieval is usually performed under sedation, though some women have cramping or soreness afterwards. Bloating or discomfort during ovarian stimulation is common. Experiences vary, and your care team will explain pain relief options.

How does age affect IVF success?

Female age is the strongest single factor: chances are generally highest under 35 and decline progressively with age, particularly after 40. Consider seeing a specialist if you have been trying to conceive for 12 months (or 6 months if over 35).
